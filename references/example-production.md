# Complete production example

This example demonstrates the delivery contract. It is not a reusable copy,
palette, font pairing, image prompt, or slide template.

## Brief

- **Request:** organic carousel about clinic leads that arrive but wait for a
  first response.
- **Role:** Diagnóstico.
- **Objective:** help a clinic manager separate acquisition from handoff before
  blaming or changing the campaign.
- **Stage:** problem-aware.
- **Message:** a generated lead and an attended lead are different operational
  events.
- **Proof:** mechanism and visibly illustrative artifacts; no client result or
  timing benchmark was supplied.
- **Action:** `PROPOSTA DE CTA` — save the handoff audit.
- **Format:** 1080 × 1440, seven slides.

## Visual preflight

**Visual thesis**

> A cobalt paper ticket represents one lead moving across a clinic reception.
> It enters the scene, stops beside an unanswered phone, becomes an audit
> artifact, and reaches a named owner only in the final frame. The audience sees
> the handoff stall instead of merely reading that response speed matters.

**Datta spine**

- Exact Datta symbol `assets/datta/datta-simbolo.svg` appears once on the cover;
  the exact mono lockup `assets/datta/datta-lockup-mono.svg` appears on the final
  slide.
- Labels progress from `OBSERVAÇÃO` to `HIPÓTESE` to `DECISÃO`.
- Documentary, illustrative, and mockup status remain explicit.
- A thin measurement bracket marks the unverified interval without inventing a
  duration.

**Expressive world**

- Porcelain `#F3F0E7`, ink `#171A20`, cobalt `#2847D7`, coral `#E45D4F`, and
  muted sage `#B6C9B6`.
- Georgia Bold for display, Segoe UI Variable for body, and Cascadia Mono for
  observation labels. Record availability/licensing in the production tool;
  none is presented as an official Datta font.
- Natural reception materials, close photographic crops, paper artifacts, and
  asymmetric editorial layouts. No green overlay.
- Official Datta palette and type are not selected for this concept.

**Image program**

1. `Image-led`: an empty clinic reception, cobalt ticket, and unanswered phone.
2. `Artifact-led`: a handoff sheet with blank timestamps and named fields,
   visibly labeled as a teaching mockup.
3. `Contextual transformation`: the same ticket moves from the desk edge to a
   stopped close-up, becomes a route marker, then reaches an assigned owner.

**Silhouette rhythm**

Full bleed → asymmetric split → overhead artifact → extreme macro → horizontal
contact strip → vertical decision route → transformed return scene.

## Carousel

### S01 — observation · image-led

**Art copy**

> O lead chegou.
> A resposta, não.

**Visual:** qualifying licensed-stock clinic reception candidate to be selected
before assembly. The chosen frame must contain a desk, dark unbranded phone,
empty chair, free desk surface, and quiet wall area; an original cobalt paper
ticket is composited at the desk edge. Exact
`assets/datta/datta-simbolo.svg` appears small in a quiet corner.

**Asset role:** make the missing handoff physically visible.

**Status:** `CENA ILUSTRATIVA / SEM DADOS DE CLIENTE`.

### S02 — distinction · split composition

**Art copy**

> Lead gerado não é lead atendido.

Support: `Entre os dois existe uma operação.`

**Visual:** a narrow crop of the reception occupies one third. The remaining
field shows `FORMULÁRIO → DISTRIBUIÇÃO → 1ª TENTATIVA`; a bracket spans the
unmeasured interval without a number.

### S03 — artifact · overhead composition

**Art copy**

> O handoff precisa deixar rastro.

**Visual:** an original handoff sheet photographed from above, with blank fields
for `ENTRADA`, `RESPONSÁVEL`, `1ª TENTATIVA`, `RESPOSTA`, and `STATUS`. Label it
`MODELO ILUSTRATIVO`, not a screenshot or client record.

### S04 — hypothesis · extreme macro

**Art copy**

> Sem horário e responsável, o gargalo vira opinião.

**Visual:** close crop of the same cobalt ticket stopped under a clear document
clip. An out-of-focus wall clock supplies context but no legible time. Label:
`HIPÓTESE — CONFIRMAR COM CRM E REGISTROS DE CONTATO`.

### S05 — diagnostic contexts · horizontal strip

**Art copy**

> Descubra onde a conversa para.

**Visual:** three environment crops, not generic cards: phone at the distribution
desk; assigned chair/owner; appointment book area. Captions: `ANTES DA
DISTRIBUIÇÃO`, `ANTES DA TENTATIVA`, `DEPOIS DA RESPOSTA`.

### S06 — method · vertical route

**Art copy**

> Audite quatro passagens.

1. `entrada → distribuição`
2. `distribuição → tentativa`
3. `tentativa → resposta`
4. `resposta → agendamento`

**Visual:** the ticket becomes a route marker moving through four vertically
spaced stations. No durations, scores, or client numbers appear.

### S07 — decision · transformed return

**Art copy**

> Antes de mudar a campanha, localize o handoff.

Support: `PROPOSTA DE CTA — Salve este roteiro para a próxima auditoria.`

**Visual:** return to a different crop of the opening licensed photograph. The
original ticket/route overlay that was stalled on S04 now reaches an endpoint
labeled `RESPONSÁVEL`; the change is clearly illustrative rather than a claim
about the photographed location. Exact mono Datta lockup
`assets/datta/datta-lockup-mono.svg` signs the piece.

## Caption

O formulário enviado registra uma entrada. Não registra, sozinho, o que
aconteceu depois.

Para separar mídia de atendimento, preserve o rastro entre entrada,
distribuição, primeira tentativa, resposta e agendamento. Horário, responsável,
canal e status tornam cada passagem auditável.

Este carrossel usa uma metáfora visual e um modelo ilustrativo. Não apresenta
tempo ideal, resultado ou dado de cliente.

Antes de mudar a campanha, localize onde o handoff interrompe.

## Asset and provenance plan

### Source route

1. No supplied clinic photography or previously licensed campaign asset is
   available in this example.
2. Search at most two appropriate free banks for one reception image family,
   using the source brief below. Run no more than two targeted rounds.
3. Select only a candidate that passes semantic necessity, composition,
   technical quality, distinctiveness/continuity, and rights. Record its direct
   asset page and current official license.
4. If no candidate passes, record `NO QUALIFYING STOCK` and generate one
   controlled reception scene as the fallback. Do not generate before this
   result.

### Image A — opening reception

- **Preferred class:** licensed stock.
- **Use:** S01 and derived crops in S02, S04, S05, and S07.
- **Purpose:** show a lead present while the handoff owner is absent.
- **Framing:** eye-level 3:4 reception desk with an unbranded phone, free desk
  surface in the lower third for the owned ticket overlay, and text-safe wall
  area. The stock photo does not need to contain a ticket.
- **Light/material:** diffuse morning light, real laminate, paper fibers, minor
  scuffs, plausible object placement.
- **Negative constraints:** text, logos, visible patient information, luxury
  clinic styling, identifiable patients, posed smiling professional, fake UI,
  or implied endorsement by the photographed clinic/person.
- **Status:** illustrative.

The selection record includes provider, access modality, direct asset-page URL,
asset ID/slug, creator, current official license URL and access date, intended
organic use, depicted-rights check, downloaded-file hash, and planned
crops/grade.

### Image-derived transformation

- **Class:** licensed Image A plus owned layout artifact.
- **Use:** stopped macro on S04, diagnostic crops on S05, and resolved route on
  S07.
- **Purpose:** make the unowned interval visible, then resolve it without
  pretending the photographed clinic performed the illustrated process.
- **Status:** illustrative composite.

### Artifact D — handoff sheet

- **Class:** owned layout artifact.
- **Use:** S03 and route source for S06.
- **Rights/source:** original layout; exact text and ticket are added during
  composition, never baked into the stock image.
- **Status:** teaching mockup, not documentary evidence.

If the stock route fails and generation is triggered, preserve the failed
candidate gates plus tool, prompt, date, edit notes, and final inspection.

## Alt text

Seven-slide carousel uses a cobalt paper ticket to represent a clinic lead. The
ticket arrives beside an unanswered phone, appears on an illustrative handoff
sheet, stops near an unreadable clock, moves through four audit stages, and is
finally received by an assigned owner. The piece contains no client data or
performance claim.

## QA verdict

`APROVADO PARA RASCUNHO`

- The visual thesis is specific to a clinic reception handoff.
- Image-led, artifact-led, and contextual-transformation anchors occur on
  different slides.
- Seven slides use seven assigned silhouettes rather than one repeated template.
- The exact Datta marks and evidence behavior carry recognition without forcing
  official palette or typography.
- All images have a semantic role and provenance plan.
- Final approval remains blocked until the selected stock is downloaded with a
  complete rights record, assembled, and inspected at full and phone-preview
  sizes; if fallback generation is triggered, its provenance is also required.
