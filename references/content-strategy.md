# Content strategy

## Core editorial system

Every Datta piece takes one role in the sequence **Sinal → Diagnóstico → Prova
→ Método → Decisão**. This is a system of reader jobs, not a fixed publishing
order or visual template.

| Role | Reader job | Useful material | Typical action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sinal | Notice a costly belief or blind spot | Sharp observation, contrast, one real fact | Pause, reflect, share |
| Diagnóstico | Understand what is actually causing the problem | Symptom vs. cause, audit logic, annotated evidence | Save, self-assess |
| Prova | Believe the mechanism works in context | Case, before/after, artifact, qualified metric | View case, request analysis |
| Método | See how Datta works and what it requires | Tracking chain, decision tree, implementation stages | Compare approaches |
| Decisão | Take one concrete next step | Offer, proof, fit criteria, destination | Contact, schedule, visit |

Use one primary role per piece. A carousel can move across adjacent roles, but
the CTA follows the role reached on its last slide.

## Subject territories

Choose topics from real Datta competence or supplied evidence:

- Paid traffic economics: budget allocation, creative, audience, auction,
  marginal return, lead quality, and decision cadence.
- Tracking and attribution: pixels, CAPI, GTM, UTMs, CRM, offline events,
  duplicate events, missing journeys, and source reliability.
- Dashboards and diagnosis: signal versus noise, metric definitions, period,
  source coverage, comparison errors, and decision design.
- Sales journey: handoff, speed-to-lead, follow-up, qualification, conversion,
  and the boundary between media and commercial execution.
- Cases and operating proof: what changed, what did not, what was measured, and
  what can reasonably be attributed.
- Buyer education: how to evaluate an agency, an audit, a dashboard, or a
  tracking project without choosing by appearance alone.

Do not force a cultural reference into a weak business connection. A trend is
usable only when the agent can finish this sentence concretely: “For this
audience, this event reveals that ___, which changes the decision about ___.”

## Angle selection

Before drafting, score the idea informally against four questions:

1. **Specificity:** Does it name a recognizable operating situation?
2. **Consequence:** Does the reader understand what the issue costs or changes?
3. **Evidence:** Is there a source, mechanism, artifact, or clearly labeled
   illustration that can support it?
4. **Decision:** Can the reader do or evaluate something different afterward?

If only the topic is present, there is not yet a post. Turn it into an angle.

Weak: “A importância do tracking.”

Usable: “Seu CPL caiu. Seu custo por venda subiu. Sem o CRM, o Meta não mostra
essa diferença.”

## Hook families

Select a hook because it fits the argument, not because it sounds dramatic.

| Family | Shape | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Costly contrast | `X melhorou. O negócio piorou.` | Both sides must be possible and later explained |
| Diagnostic question | `Você mede X. Consegue ligar isso a Y?` | The body must answer the question |
| Misleading signal | `O número está certo. A leitura está errada.` | Show the missing context |
| Operational consequence | `O lead chegou. A origem desapareceu.` | Name where the chain breaks |
| Evidence-first | `Periodo + metric + what changed` | Include source and attribution limit |

Avoid borrowed shells such as “a morte de...”, “o que X ensina sobre...”,
“existe paz em...”, or “não somos para qualquer empresa”. Write the insight in
Datta's own operational language.

## Proof ladder

Use the strongest proof actually available, never the strongest proof that
would be convenient.

1. **Business outcome:** revenue, qualified sales, margin, CAC, payback.
2. **Commercial outcome:** opportunities, show rate, conversion, lead quality.
3. **Media outcome:** spend, reach, CTR, CPL, frequency.
4. **Implementation evidence:** event coverage, source continuity, dashboard,
   campaign artifact, or QA log.
5. **Mechanism:** a technically correct explanation.
6. **Hypothetical illustration:** teaching only, visibly labeled.

Do not present a lower rung as if it proved a higher one. Reach does not prove
revenue; a mockup does not prove physical placement; a dashboard screenshot
does not prove causation.

## Feed coherence without template repetition

Maintain recognition through tokens, measurement details, voice, and evidence
handling while rotating:

- editorial role;
- image-led, evidence-led, and type-led composition;
- dark and approved light fields;
- static, short carousel, deep carousel, case, and ad;
- documentary, conceptual, and artifact-based imagery.

If the last three proposed pieces use the same canvas mode, hero arrangement,
or hook family, change the next one unless the campaign deliberately needs a
series system.
